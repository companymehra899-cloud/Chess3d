Short description (max 80 characters):

Full description:
Chess 3D Arena is a 3D chess game for Android.

Play ranked and casual games, solve puzzles, challenge a bot, and track your rating on the leaderboard.

Features:
- 3D chessboard and pieces
- Online matchmaking
- Bot games with multiple time controls
- Daily-style puzzles
- Friends, chat during games, and match history
- Custom avatars, boards, and piece styles
- Guest play or local username sign-in

No ads. Built for players who want a clean chess experience.
