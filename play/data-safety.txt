Play Console Data safety answers for Chess 3D Arena

Data collected:
- User IDs: yes, local username and Firebase anonymous UID for online play
- Game progress: yes, rating, wins, losses, puzzles, match history
- Messages: yes, in-game chat during online matches
- App activity: game interactions needed to play chess

Data shared:
- Other players in a match can see display name, rating, country, avatar, moves, and chat
- Firebase/Google processes online matchmaking and game sync data

Security:
- Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) for online features
- Users can request deletion by clearing app data or uninstalling
- Passwords are hashed on device

Not collected:
- Location
- Contacts
- Photos
- Files
- Financial info
- Health info
- Advertising ID (do not collect)

Ads: no
In-app purchases: no
Targeted advertising: no
Data sold: no
Children: not primarily directed at children
