Privacy Policy for Chess 3D Arena

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Chess 3D Arena ("the app") handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Information we collect

The app may store the following information on your device:

  • Username and a hashed password for local sign-in
  • Player profile details such as display name, country, avatar, rating, tokens, and settings
  • Local match history, puzzle progress, and friend list

If you use online matchmaking, the app uses Firebase Authentication (anonymous sign-in) and Firebase Realtime Database to store matchmaking queue data, live game moves, timers, and in-game chat messages needed to run a match.

2. How we use information

  • To create and restore your local player profile
  • To run offline games, puzzles, leaderboards, and settings
  • To match you with other players and sync an online chess game

3. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Online match data is processed by Google Firebase to provide multiplayer features. Other players in a match may see your display name, rating, country, and avatar.

4. Data storage and security

Local profile and account data are stored in an on-device database. Passwords are stored as SHA-256 hashes, not as plain text. Online game data is stored in Firebase Realtime Database while a match is active.

5. Permissions

The app requests Internet and network state access to support online play and Firebase services. Offline play, puzzles, and local accounts work without an account on our servers.

6. Children

The app is a general-audience chess game. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

You can play as a guest, change your display name, and clear app data from Android settings to remove locally stored information. Uninstalling the app removes local data from the device.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/companymehra899-cloud/Chess3d
